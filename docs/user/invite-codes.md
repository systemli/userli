# Invite codes

New accounts can only be created with an invite code. As a user, you can
receive invite codes to invite your friends.

![Invite codes](../assets/images/voucher.png){class="screenshot"}

## When do I get invite codes?

Your provider decides per domain whether invitations are possible, how many
invite codes each user gets and how long the waiting period is. By default,
users get **3** invite codes **7 days** after registration.

You get your invite codes when

* invitations are enabled for your domain,
* your account is older than the waiting period, and
* you have signed in at least once.

Open **Invite codes** in the navigation to see them. If you still have to wait,
the page shows the date when you get your codes. The number is a total – used
codes are not replaced.

If the menu entry is missing, invitations are disabled for your domain or your
account is not allowed to invite others.

## Invite a friend

Click **Copy to clipboard** next to an unused code. This copies a complete
registration link like `https://users.example.org/register/aB3xY9`. Send it to
your friend. They can open it and [create an account](register.md).

Each code can be used once. Used codes show the date they were redeemed.

!!! warning "Invite people you trust"

    You are responsible for the people you invite. If accounts are misused for
    spam, your provider may also block the invite codes of the person who
    invited them.
