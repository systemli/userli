# Mail app passwords

![Mail app passwords](../assets/images/app-passwords.png){class="screenshot"}

A mail app password is a separate password for one email program, for example
Thunderbird on your laptop or the mail app on your phone. Use it instead of
your password when you set up the app.

Mail app passwords work for reading and sending email (IMAP, SMTP) and for the
webmail. They do **not** work for signing in to this web interface or to other
services. If a device gets lost, delete its mail app password. Your password and
your other devices keep working.

## Create a mail app password

![Mail app passwords card in the account settings](../assets/images/app-passwords-settings.png){class="screenshot"}

1. Go to **Account settings → Mail App Passwords**.
2. Enter a **Name** that tells you where you use it, for example
   *Thunderbird on laptop*. Each name can be used only once.
3. Enter your current password to confirm it's you and click **Create mail
   app password**. The mail app password itself is generated for you.
4. Copy the new mail app password and enter it in your email program instead of
   your password.

![New mail app password](../assets/images/app-passwords-created.png){class="screenshot"}

!!! warning

    The mail app password is shown only once. If you lose it, delete it and create
    a new one.

## Delete a mail app password

Go to **Account settings → Mail App Passwords** and click **Delete** next to the
mail app password. The app using it can't sign in anymore.

Changing your password does not delete your mail app passwords. If you reset
your password with your recovery token, your provider resets your account or you
delete your account, all mail app passwords are deleted.
