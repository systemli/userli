# User Guide

This guide is for people who have an email account on a service that runs
Userli – or who want to get one. You don't need any technical knowledge.

!!! info "Names and addresses differ"

    Userli is used by different email providers. Your provider may use its own
    name and web address instead of "Userli". Menus and buttons are the same.
    In this guide, `example.org` stands for the domain of your provider.

## Reading and writing emails

Userli manages your account, but it is not an email program. To read and write
emails, either

* use the **Webmail** of your provider (if available, there is a *Webmail*
  card on your account overview), or
* set up an email program such as [Thunderbird](https://www.thunderbird.net/),
  K-9 Mail or the mail app on your phone. Ask your provider for the server
  settings (IMAP and SMTP server names and ports). Use your full email address
  as username and your Userli password.

## Your privacy and security in short

* **Choose a strong, unique password.** It must have at least 12 characters
  and must not appear in known data breaches.
* **Store your recovery token** in a safe place. It is the only way back into
  your account if you forget your password – your provider cannot reset it
  for you without losing your encrypted emails.
* **Turn on two-factor authentication** to protect your account even if your
  password gets stolen.

## Need help?

If something does not work as described, contact the support of your email
provider. If you are locked out after a spam warning, only your provider can
unlock your account.
