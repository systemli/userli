# Aliases

An alias is an additional email address. Emails to an alias arrive in your
normal inbox. Aliases help you to keep your main address private and to see
who passed on your address.

![Aliases](../assets/images/alias.png){class="screenshot"}

Go to **Aliases** in the navigation. All aliases use the same domain as your
account.

## Custom aliases

Custom aliases have a name you choose, for example `newsletter@example.org`.

* You can create up to **3** custom aliases.
* Allowed are lowercase letters, digits and `-`, `_` and `.`.
* The name must not be in use or reserved.
* **Custom aliases can't be deleted by you.** Choose carefully. If you need
  one removed, contact your provider.

To create one, enter the name under *Create new custom alias* and click
**Add**.

## Random aliases

Random aliases have a generated name of 24 letters and digits, for example
`k3j9x0q2m7...@example.org`. They are ideal for online shops, newsletters and
sign-ups.

* You can have up to **100** random aliases.
* Add an optional **note** (up to 40 characters) to remember what you used the
  alias for.
* Delete a random alias at any time with **Delete alias**. After that, emails to
  this address are no longer delivered. A deleted address can't be used again.

To create one, optionally enter a note and click
**Generate random alias address**.

## Good to know

* You receive an email whenever a new alias is created for your account.
* To **send** emails from an alias, set it up as an additional identity in your
  email program or webmail. Most providers allow sending from your own
  aliases.
* To let others find your OpenPGP key for a custom alias, see
  [OpenPGP keys](openpgp.md).
